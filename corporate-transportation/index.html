How companies book with us

Most corporate rides are booked by assistants and travel coordinators, so the process is short and confirmed in writing.

One message to book

Predictable pricing

Corporate questions

Before you book

Can our company set up an account?

Yes. Regular clients book by email or WhatsApp with one message per ride and receive one confirmation back. Monthly invoicing is available for regular accounts.

Can an assistant book on behalf of an executive?

Yes. Most of our corporate rides are booked by executive assistants and travel coordinators. Send the passenger name, pickup, drop-off and times, and the chauffeur details go to whoever you choose.

Do you handle groups and delegations?

Yes. For boards, delegations and incentive groups we run several vehicles together, each seating six with luggage, with one point of contact and one bill.

Which areas do you cover for business travel?

Scottsdale, Paradise Valley, Phoenix, Tempe, Chandler, Mesa, Gilbert and Glendale, plus every Valley airport: PHX Sky Harbor, Scottsdale Airport (SDL), Phoenix-Mesa Gateway and Deer Valley.
