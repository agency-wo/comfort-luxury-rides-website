How hourly works

How hourly service works

Point-to-point rides are priced by the journey. Hourly service is priced by time, and the chauffeur and vehicle stay with you for all of it.

  1. Tell us the start and a rough plan

    Start time, pickup address, roughly how long and roughly where. We quote one hourly rate and confirm the hours with you before anything is booked.

  2. The chauffeur stays with you

    Between stops the car waits where you left it, with the air running and your bags locked inside. There is no re-booking at each stop.

  3. Extend on the day

    If the day runs long, tell the chauffeur and the booking extends at the same rate, subject to availability.

Hourly questions

Before you book

Is there a minimum number of hours?

Hourly bookings are quoted per request. Tell us the start time and a rough plan for the day, and we confirm the hours and the rate together before you commit.

Can we go outside Scottsdale and Phoenix?

Yes. Day trips to Sedona, Tucson, Jerome, the Apache Trail and other Arizona destinations are quoted on request as hourly or as a fixed day rate, whichever suits the plan better.

Can the chauffeur make stops and wait?

Yes. Within the booked time the chauffeur makes as many stops as you like and waits at each one, with your bags locked in the vehicle.
