Airport pickups

How a pickup works

What happens between landing and getting into the car.

  1. Send the flight number

    We follow the arrival time and move the pickup with it. If the flight is early or late, the car is still at the terminal when you land.

  2. Walk out to the meeting point

    Your chauffeur texts the pickup spot before you land: the arrivals curb outside baggage claim, or inside the terminal if you prefer. Luggage goes in the cargo bay.

  3. Ride home at the price you were quoted

    One flat price, agreed when you booked. No meter and no surge pricing during Spring Training or on a holiday weekend.

The airports

The airports we serve

PHXPhoenix Sky Harbor International

The main airport for the Valley, about 25 minutes from central Scottsdale outside peak traffic. Both terminals, 3 and 4, are covered. Curbside pickup is standard; tell us if you want your chauffeur inside at baggage claim.

SDLScottsdale Airport and private aviation

One of the busiest private-jet airports in the country, minutes from North Scottsdale, Kierland and the resorts along Scottsdale Road. We collect at the FBO terminals, including Signature, Atlantic and Jet Aviation, and coordinate the timing with your crew or handler.

AZAPhoenix-Mesa Gateway

The East Valley airport, about 35 miles from Scottsdale. Popular for low-cost carriers and for Chandler, Gilbert and Mesa pickups, and quoted at a flat rate.

DVTPhoenix Deer Valley

General aviation in North Phoenix, close to Desert Ridge, Cave Creek and Carefree. Private arrivals are met on the ramp side or at the terminal, whichever your operator prefers.

Airport questions

Before you book

Do you track my flight?

Yes. Give us the flight number when you book and we time the pickup to the actual arrival. If the flight lands early or late, the car moves with it.

Where do I meet my chauffeur at PHX Sky Harbor?

Your chauffeur texts the exact pickup point before you land: the arrivals curb outside your terminal's baggage claim, or a meet inside the terminal if you ask for it. Terminals 3 and 4 are both covered.

Can you pick up at Scottsdale Airport's private terminals?

Yes. We collect from the FBO terminals at Scottsdale Airport (SDL), including Signature, Atlantic and Jet Aviation, and coordinate the pickup time with your flight crew or handler.

Is the airport price a flat rate?

Yes. Every airport transfer is quoted as one flat price before you book, based on the pickup address and the airport. There is no meter and no surge pricing at busy times.

How much does a car service from Scottsdale to PHX cost?

Every airport transfer is a flat price based on the pickup area, the time and the number of vehicles, so we do not publish a rate card. Send the pickup address and the flight and we reply with the price, usually within the hour. The price you are quoted is the price you pay.
