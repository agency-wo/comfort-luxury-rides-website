The company

What we do and how we work

Airport transfers, corporate travel, event transportation and hourly chauffeur service, all in the same class of vehicle and to the same four commitments.

Punctuality

The chauffeur arrives before the pickup time. Airport pickups are timed to the flight, not to the schedule printed on the ticket.

Privacy

What is said in the car stays in the car. The chauffeur does not make conversation unless you start it, and does not discuss clients.

Exclusivity

One client per booking. The vehicle is yours for the transfer or for the hours you book, and it does not pick anyone else up on the way.

Professionalism

Business attire, a clean vehicle, and a route planned before the pickup. The price is agreed in writing before the ride.

Leadership

Ergi Janku, CEO and Managing Director

Ergi founded Comfort Luxury Rides in 2020 and runs it day to day: the quotes, the bookings and the standard every chauffeur works to.

Because the company is small and owner-run, the person who quotes your ride is the person responsible for it. Questions about a booking reach the owner, not a call centre.
