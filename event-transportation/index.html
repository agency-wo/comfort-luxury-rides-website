Venues and events

Where we take you

Stadiums, ballparks, golf tournaments, resorts and Old Town. We know the drop zones and the pickup points at each.

Game day

State Farm Stadium for the Cardinals in Glendale, Footprint Center for the Suns and Chase Field for the Diamondbacks downtown, and Spring Training at Salt River Fields, Scottsdale Stadium and the Cactus League ballparks. Drop at the gate, pickup at the gate.

WM Phoenix Open and Barrett-Jackson

TPC Scottsdale every winter for the Open, WestWorld for the collector-car auction. The flat price is the same on the busiest weekend of the year.

Weddings

Couples, parents and out-of-town guests moved between hotel, ceremony and reception across Scottsdale, Paradise Valley and Phoenix, timed to the ceremony and the reception.

Nights out in Old Town

Dinner in Old Town Scottsdale, a bar on Saddlebag Trail, then home to North Scottsdale or Tempe. Book by the hour and the car stays with you; book point to point and we come back when you call.

Concerts, shows and galas

Talking Stick Resort, Arizona Financial Theatre, Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale, and the resort galas and charity dinners along Scottsdale Road and Camelback.

Golf groups and celebrations

Tee times at Troon North, Grayhawk and TPC with the golf bags in the cargo bay. Birthdays, bachelor and bachelorette parties and anniversaries, with nobody needing to drive.

Event questions

Before you book

Do you drive to Cardinals, Suns and Diamondbacks games?

Yes. State Farm Stadium in Glendale, Footprint Center and Chase Field downtown, and Spring Training ballparks including Salt River Fields and Scottsdale Stadium. We drop at the gate and pick up at an agreed point when the game ends.

Can you take us to the WM Phoenix Open and Barrett-Jackson?

Yes. TPC Scottsdale for the Open and WestWorld for Barrett-Jackson, every winter. The flat price is the same on those weekends as on any other. Book early, because those dates fill up.

Do you do weddings?

Yes. Couples, parents and guests between hotel, ceremony and reception across Scottsdale, Paradise Valley and Phoenix, timed to the day. For wedding parties larger than six we send more than one vehicle.

Is it better to book by the hour for a night out?

For a night with several stops, yes: the chauffeur waits between venues and the car stays with you. For one drop-off and one pickup, point to point is simpler and priced per transfer.
